Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about corporate ordering with Giftxindia.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ for standard pre-curated collections with logo customization is 25 units. For completely custom-molded boxes, the MOQ is 50 units.

Can you ship directly to individual employee home addresses?

Yes. You provide an encrypted Excel file with recipient addresses and phone numbers. We manage individual dispatches and provide tracking dashboards.

How fast can I get a physical sample?

Digital mockups are sent within 4 hours. Once approved, physical samples are dispatched via express courier and reach major Indian metros in 48-72 hours.

Are GST invoices provided for corporate tax claims?

Yes, 100% of our orders include formal GST invoices with your company's GSTIN and state code for standard input tax credits.
